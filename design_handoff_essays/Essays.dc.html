No. 06Strategy6 min readWhen the people who run the process can change the softwareOperators can now describe a tool and have a coding agent build it in an afternoon. The speed is real. No. 07Engineering6 min readDefine done before you buildPrompts drift and model runs vary. An ordered list of events, each with a proof that can be checked against the real system, gives an AI workflow a finish line that holds across rewrites, model changes and restarts. No. 08Engineering6 min readA workshop, not the vaultAI systems stall when they cannot provision, test or verify their own work. Handing them production access is the wrong fix. Give them a complete environment of their own, and let only reviewed changes cross into yours.
